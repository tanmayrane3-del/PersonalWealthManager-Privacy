Personal Wealth Manager

Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 18, 2026

1. Overview

Personal Wealth Manager ("the App") is a personal finance tracking application for Android. This policy explains what data we collect, why we collect it, and how it is used.

We do not sell, share, or monetize your data. We do not use analytics or advertising SDKs of any kind.

2. Data We Collect and Why

2.1 SMS Messages
Permissions: READ_SMS, RECEIVE_SMS

The App reads incoming SMS messages only from recognized bank senders (e.g. ICICI, SBI, Kotak, Indusind, Bank of Baroda) to automatically create expense records. SMS content is never uploaded to any third party. Only bank transaction SMS are processed — all others are ignored. Parsed transaction data is stored locally on your device only.

2.2 Account Credentials

Your email address and session token are stored locally on your device to keep you signed in. Your Zerodha API Key and Secret are transmitted to our backend server solely to fetch your stock holdings. They are not stored on our servers beyond the active session.

2.3 Financial Data

The App stores the following locally on your device:

  • Bank transaction records (parsed from bank SMS)
  • Stock holdings fetched from Zerodha
  • Gold and metals portfolio data entered manually

2.4 Notifications
Permission: POST_NOTIFICATIONS

Used only to notify you when a bank SMS has been automatically recorded as a transaction.

3. Data Storage

Data Where Stored Encrypted
Session token Local device (SharedPreferences) No
Email address Local device (SharedPreferences) No
Transaction records Local device (Room database) No
Zerodha credentials Transmitted to backend; not stored locally N/A

Your data is not synced to any cloud service except through our backend API (hosted at wealth-backend-demo.onrender.com) which handles authentication and Zerodha data fetching. Encryption of locally stored data is planned for a future update.

4. Data We Do NOT Collect

  • Location data
  • Contacts, camera, or microphone
  • Advertising or analytics data
  • Usage behavior or tracking
  • Personal (non-bank) SMS messages

No other third-party services are used.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

All financial data is stored locally on your device. Uninstalling the App removes all locally stored data. To request deletion of any data held on our backend server, contact us at the email below.

7. Children's Privacy

This App is not intended for users under 18 years of age. We do not knowingly collect data from minors.
