1. Overview

Personal Wealth Manager ("the App") is a personal finance tracking application for Android. This policy explains what data we collect, where it is stored, who it is shared with, and how you can have it deleted.

The App is account-based and server-backed. You create an account, and your financial records are stored on our backend server so they are available across sessions and devices. The App is not an offline-only tool.

We do not sell or rent your data. We do not use advertising or analytics SDKs of any kind.

2. Data We Collect and Why

2.1 Account Information

When you register, we collect and store on our server your name, email address, and phone number, together with a password used to authenticate you. We also record your IP address and browser/device user-agent against each login session, in order to manage sessions and detect unauthorised access.

2.2 Bank SMS Messages

Permissions: READ_SMS, RECEIVE_SMS

With your permission, the App reads incoming SMS messages in order to create expense and income records automatically, so you do not have to enter every transaction by hand.

Which messages are read. Only messages whose sender ID matches a recognised bank shortcode are processed. At present these are ICICI, SBI, HDFC, Kotak, IndusInd, Bank of Baroda, and Federal Bank. Messages from any other sender — personal messages, OTPs from other services, promotional SMS — are discarded on your device and never leave it.

Important — please read. To extract the amount, date, merchant and account details from a bank SMS, the full text of that message, along with the sender ID and the time it was received, is transmitted from your device to our backend server, and from there to Anthropic's Claude API, which performs the extraction. This means the content of your bank SMS messages leaves your device and is processed by a third-party service provider. It is not used to show you advertising, and it is not sold.

If your device is offline or the server cannot be reached, the message is held in a queue in the App's local database on your device and retried later. Once processed successfully, the queued copy is marked complete.

SMS reading is optional. If you decline the permission, the App remains fully usable and you can add transactions manually.

2.3 Financial Records

The following are stored on our backend server and linked to your account:

  • Income and expense transactions, including amount, date, category, source and recipient
  • Budget plans, monthly allocations and spending against them
  • Stock holdings retrieved from Zerodha
  • Mutual fund holdings, including data imported from CAS statements
  • Gold, silver and other precious-metal holdings
  • Other assets and liabilities you record
  • Net worth snapshots calculated over time
  • Your custom categories, payment sources and recipients

2.4 Zerodha Broker Credentials

If you choose to connect a Zerodha account, your Zerodha API key, API secret and the access token issued by Zerodha are stored on our backend server so that your holdings can be refreshed without you re-authenticating each time. Connecting Zerodha is entirely optional, and you may disconnect it at any time.

2.5 Notifications

Permission: POST_NOTIFICATIONS

Used only to tell you when a bank SMS has been recorded as a transaction. We do not send marketing notifications.

3. Where Your Data Is Stored

Data Where it is stored Additional field-level encryption
Name, email, phone Backend database No
Session token, email, name Your device (SharedPreferences) No
Login IP address and user-agent Backend database No
Transactions, budgets, holdings, net worth Backend database No
Bank SMS content Sent to backend and to Anthropic for parsing; queued temporarily on your device when offline No
Zerodha API secret and access token Backend database Yes — AES-256-GCM
Zerodha API key Backend database No — an API key is an identifier, not a secret

Two layers apply. The database as a whole is encrypted at rest by our hosting provider. On top of that, your Zerodha API secret and access token — the credentials that could act on your brokerage account — are individually encrypted by us before they are written, so they remain unreadable even to someone who obtains a copy of the database.

All communication between the App and our server uses HTTPS, and the App blocks unencrypted HTTP traffic entirely. Passwords are stored as one-way bcrypt hashes and cannot be recovered by anyone, including us. Extending field-level encryption to other stored records is planned, and this policy will be updated when it ships.

4. Data We Do NOT Collect

  • Location data
  • Contacts, camera, microphone or photos
  • Advertising identifiers, analytics or usage tracking
  • SMS messages from any sender other than the recognised bank shortcodes listed above
  • Call logs

6. Data Retention and Account Deletion

We keep your data for as long as your account exists. You can ask us to delete your account and everything associated with it at any time.

How to delete your account. The quickest way is in the app: Settings → Account → Delete Account, which takes effect immediately. If you no longer have the app installed, email tanmaypwm@gmail.com from the address registered to your account and we will confirm and complete the deletion within 30 days. Full instructions are on our account deletion page.

What gets deleted. Your account record, all transactions, budgets, holdings, mutual funds, metals, other assets, liabilities, net worth history, categories, stored bank SMS records, login sessions and any stored Zerodha credentials are permanently removed from our database.

What is kept. The payee and income-source entries you created are retained, but your account is unlinked from them first so they can no longer be traced back to you, any contact details on them are erased, and they are deactivated so they no longer appear anywhere in the app.

Uninstalling the App removes the data held on your device, but does not by itself delete the records held on our server — please use the process above for that.

7. Your Rights

You may request a copy of the data we hold about you, ask us to correct anything inaccurate, withdraw the SMS permission at any time through Android's system settings, disconnect your Zerodha account, or have your account deleted entirely. Use the contact details below for any of these.

8. Children's Privacy

This App is not intended for users under 18 years of age. We do not knowingly collect data from minors. If you believe a minor has created an account, contact us and we will remove it.

9. Changes to This Policy
