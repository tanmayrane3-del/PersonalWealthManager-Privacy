Delete Your Account

Last updated: August 9, 2026

See also: Privacy Policy  ·  Terms & Conditions

You can delete your Personal Wealth Manager account and all of the data associated with it at any time. There are two ways to do it.

1. Delete it yourself in the app

This is the fastest route and takes effect immediately.

  1. Open the Personal Wealth Manager app and sign in.
  2. Open the menu and go to Settings.
  3. Scroll to Account and tap Delete Account.
  4. Read the confirmation, enter your password, and tap Delete my account.

Your account and data are removed straight away and you are signed out.

2. Request deletion by email

If you no longer have the app installed, or cannot sign in, email us from the address registered to your account:

tanmaypwm@gmail.com

Please send it from your registered email address so we can verify the request, and put "Account deletion request" in the subject line. We will confirm and complete the deletion within 30 days.

3. What gets deleted

Everything below is permanently removed from our servers:

  • Your account, name, email address and phone number
  • All income and expense transactions
  • Budgets, monthly plans and income targets
  • Stock holdings, mutual fund holdings and precious-metal holdings
  • Other assets, liabilities and your entire net worth history
  • Your custom categories
  • Stored bank SMS records
  • Your saved Zerodha API credentials and access tokens
  • All login sessions

Deletion is permanent. Once your account is deleted the data cannot be recovered, by you or by us. If you want to keep a copy of your records, export or note them down before deleting.

4. What is kept, and why

Two things are not deleted outright:

Payee and income-source entries. The list of payees and income sources you created is kept, but your user account is unlinked from it first, so the entries can no longer be traced back to you. Any contact details stored on them are erased, and the entries are deactivated so they no longer appear anywhere in the app.

Anonymous, aggregated records. Data that never identified you — such as system logs that contain no personal information — may be retained for the operation and security of the service.

5. After deletion

Your email address is released, so you are free to create a new account with the same address later if you wish. A new account starts empty; none of your previous data is restored.

Deleting your account does not automatically revoke API access you granted at Zerodha. If you connected a Zerodha account, we recommend also removing the app's access from your Zerodha account settings.

6. Questions

If you have any questions about deleting your account or what happens to your data, contact tanmaypwm@gmail.com. Full details of what we collect and why are in our Privacy Policy.
